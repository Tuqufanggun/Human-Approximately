Body Docking Structures

Current status

Family Hypothesis

We classify these structures by a repeated physical relationship with the Human body. Each receives a predictable region of the body and holds it in a reproducible configuration.

The structures differ greatly in form and surrounding context. Humans used similar bodily configurations across otherwise unrelated activities.

Family Research Note

Across these variants, artificial structures completed, positioned or redirected the Human body.

Humans depended on constructed environments to maintain ordinary bodily states. Their structures also organised how bodies moved, received care and occupied space.
